Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Tap Rush ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed and published by Deepu Siva Private Limited. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Tap Rush is a locally-operated game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • High scores and settings are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage — they never leave your device.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

2. Advertising

Tap Rush may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

We comply with Google Play's Families Policy and do not display interest-based advertising to users under 13.

3. Analytics

We may use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand aggregate usage patterns such as session length and feature usage. This data is anonymous and not linked to any individual.

4. Children's Privacy

Tap Rush is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game contains no in-app purchases requiring payment from children.

5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may be used:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no data at risk from our servers. Your local game data is stored on your device and is protected by your device's security mechanisms.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
