Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

GameVerse ("we", "our", "the app") is developed and published by Deepu Siva Private Limited. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data across all 12 games included in the app: Tap Rush, Sky Runner, Candy Match, Coin Kingdom, Brain Blocks, Happy Farm, Sling Shot, Card Flip, Snake Legends, Word Quest, Tower Stack, and Bubble Pop.

1. Account Information

GameVerse uses a local account system. When you register:

  • Your username, password (encoded), avatar, and coin balance are stored only on your device using localStorage.
  • We do not transmit this data to any server. No cloud account exists today.
  • A future update may add optional Google Sign-In or cloud sync — you will be notified and asked for consent before that feature is enabled.

2. Game Save Data

Each game saves your progress (level, score, in-game currency) locally per account, so you can resume exactly where you left off — even after closing the app. This data never leaves your device.

3. Advertising

GameVerse may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. We comply with Google Play Families Policy.

4. Analytics

We may use Firebase Analytics to understand anonymous, aggregate usage trends (e.g. which games are most played). This data cannot identify you personally.

5. Children's Privacy

GameVerse is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond the locally-stored username/avatar described above.

6. Data Security

Because account and save data stay on your device, there is no central server to be breached. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage will permanently delete your local account and progress.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page whenever our data practices change, including when cloud sync or Google Sign-In becomes available.
